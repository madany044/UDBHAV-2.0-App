ABOUT UDBHAV-2.0

UDBHAV-2.0 is a prestigious Project Expo organized by Mysuru Royal Institute of Technology, open to all domains and innovative minds.
This event provides a platform for students, researchers, and professionals to showcase their groundbreaking projects and compete for recognition and rewards.

Whether you're working on cutting-edge technology, sustainable solutions, or innovative applications, UDBHAV-2.0 welcomes projects from all fields including Engineering, Computer Science, Biotechnology, Management, and more.
